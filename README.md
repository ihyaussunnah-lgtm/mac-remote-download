# Mac Remote

Control your Mac from your phone's browser, from anywhere.

## Download

**[⬇ Download Mac Remote for Mac](https://github.com/ihyaussunnah-lgtm/mac-remote-download/releases/latest/download/MacRemote.zip)** (macOS 14 Sonoma or newer, Apple silicon and Intel)

## Install
1. Open the downloaded `MacRemote.zip`, then drag **Mac Remote** into your **Applications** folder.
2. Open **Mac Remote**. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
3. Allow **Screen Recording** and **Accessibility** when Mac Remote asks. Use the buttons in the app, then click **Restart Mac Remote**.
4. Point your phone's camera at the QR code in the app and tap the link. To keep it on your phone, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

Every Mac gets its own private link and password. Don't share your QR code: anyone who scans it can control your Mac.
